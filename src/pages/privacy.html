Privacy

What this site keeps.

Short version: nothing about you.

On this website

  • We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The pages for guests have no forms, so we collect no names, emails or phone numbers.
  • Your browser keeps a copy of our menu and hours so the page still works if the connection drops. It holds nothing about you and you can clear it at any time.
  • The company that hosts this site, and Google, which serves our menu and hours, receive your IP address when a page loads, as every web server does.

When you leave it

Ordering and gift cards happen on Toast, reservations on Yelp, directions on Google Maps, photos on Instagram, and catering and event requests on Toast. Links that leave this site are marked with an arrow ↗. Each of those companies has its own privacy policy, and what you give them is between you and them.
